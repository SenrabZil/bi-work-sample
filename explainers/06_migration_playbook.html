Five routes,

Moving a whole Power BI estate to Fabric. The routes trade speed against long-term value; the groundwork is the same for all of them.

with Elizabeth Cecilia. Choose the route late; start the groundwork now.

01

Five routes

1 · Capacity move onlyReassign workspaces to Fabric capacity. Nothing is rebuilt. Fast and safe, but no Fabric benefits and all the old debt stays.
2 · Automated conversionTooling copies Power Query into Dataflow Gen2 and builds a Direct Lake copy of the model. Quick, but it carries the old logic across unchanged.
3 · Re-platform the dataRebuild bronze, silver and gold in a lakehouse. Keep the model and reports, then repoint them. The most long-term value, and needs data engineering.
4 · Phased hybridMove domain by domain or the biggest tables first, with Import and Direct Lake side by side. Lower risk, value arrives early, runs longer.
5 · Rebuild from scratchNew models and reports designed for Fabric. Fixes deep problems, but costs most and is hardest to prove equal to the old figures.

05

The rule, in one breath

Pick the route by deadline, risk and debt, but start the groundwork today, because every route needs it.

Inventory, profiling, version control and agreed reference figures cost little, reduce the risk of every route, and pay off even if the migration slips.
