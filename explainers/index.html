THE MODEL

Seven lessons, about ten minutes each

01One model, 53 wires.The star schema: 21 fact tables, 8 lookup tables, and why the source register is the hub.Try: Trace any lookup table or page through the real 53 relationships. 02Every number explains itself.Basis lines and explanations on every tile, and why each gets its own measure.Try: Break a shared measure by clearing its filters. 03Three rules, broken on purpose.Mixed grain, a four-way country table and a duplicated stage: reasons, costs and fixes.Try: See which figures a commodity slicer really reaches. 04The check passed. It was wrong.Version control, the validator, and a gap a review found and closed.Try: Step through the validator before and after the fix. 05Same model, new floor.Moving the model to Fabric: lakehouse layers, Import or Direct Lake, and data apps.Try: Choose a storage mode per table group; sort jobs between report and app. 06Five routes, one groundwork.Migrating a whole Power BI estate: five routes compared, and the work every route needs.Try: Rank the routes against deadline, risk, debt and capacity. 07Show it once. Then let it repeat.How a coding agent built the model under rules, and what it gets wrong.Try: Step through the loop, and see what breaks if a step is skipped.

The model is a minerals and metals intelligence mock-up built in Power BI. Its structure, counts and checks are real, taken from its project files. The figures on its report pages are illustrative.
